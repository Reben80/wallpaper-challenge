# Wallpaper Group Challenge

A team classroom game for identifying the wallpaper group of Islamic geometric patterns. Teams answer the yes/no questions of the classification flowchart one at a time. A correct answer earns 1 point. The last question of a pattern can be worth 1, 2 or 3 points (set on the start screen).

**Play:** https://reben80.github.io/wallpaper-challenge/

Made by Dr. Rebin Muhammad (MathArtPlay).

## Credits

- Pattern images and their wallpaper-group classifications come from Brian Wichmann, *Symmetry in Islamic Geometric Art* (2020), and [tilingsearch.org](http://www.tilingsearch.org).
- The question sequence follows the flowchart at [wallpapergroup.netlify.app](https://wallpapergroup.netlify.app), which is based on David Richeson's diagram.
